  ALGORITHMS COMPARED:
    1. Dijkstra — greedy, expands lowest-cost first.
       LIMITATION: cannot take a loss now for a bigger gain later
       because it never revisits cheaper-looking states.
    2. Bellman-Ford SSSP — relaxes ALL edges at each depth.
       CAN take an intermediate loss if the next step profits more.
       Guaranteed optimal within the depth limit.
    3. Bellman-Ford cycle detection — finds risk-free loops.

  RECOMMENDATION for volatile markets:
    Use BF-SSSP as the primary algorithm. It finds paths Dijkstra misses
    where you lose money on an intermediate swap/transfer but gain more
    on the next step (e.g., pay gas to bridge ETH to a chain where it's
    1.7% more expensive, even though the bridge itself costs 0.06%).
