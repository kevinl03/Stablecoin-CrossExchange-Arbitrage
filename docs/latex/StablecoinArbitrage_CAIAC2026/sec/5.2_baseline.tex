\subsection{Ablation Studies}

We isolate heuristic contributions via three ablation baselines: (1)~\textbf{Cycle ablation} using Bellman--Ford negative-cycle detection; (2)~\textbf{Depth ablation} with \texttt{simple\_1hop} (single action) and \texttt{simple\_2hop} (minimal two-edge path); (3)~\textbf{Heuristic ablation} setting $h(n)=0$ (Dijkstra). Bellman--Ford detects whether negative-weight cycles exist in the log-transformed graph but does not account for slippage, latency, or reliability, and fails to produce executable paths under realistic frictions. The shallow baselines fail across all start nodes, confirming that profitable stablecoin arbitrage requires $\geq$3 hops. Results are in Table~\ref{tab:baseline-performance}.
