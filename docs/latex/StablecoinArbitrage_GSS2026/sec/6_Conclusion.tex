We presented an execution-aware A* framework for cross-exchange stablecoin arbitrage, evaluated on a unique centralized exchange (CEX) stablecoin dataset spanning 12~exchanges. $h_2$ (slippage) reduces expansions by 29\% while matching Dijkstra's profit; $h_1$ and $h_3$ overcompensate risk, expanding more nodes for less profit. Future work includes asynchronous order-book pre-fetching and extension to DEX/AMM markets.
