Most prior work models arbitrage as negative-weight cycle detection via Bellman--Ford\textsuperscript{\cite{bellman1958routing,cormen2009introduction,oanta2023crypto,defiposer2021,mmbf2024,rich2025,fang2022cryptocurrency}}, focusing on opportunity identification rather than execution feasibility. Cross-exchange settings introduce blockchain latency, withdrawal fees, and liquidity fragmentation\textsuperscript{\cite{obadia2021flashbots,optimalexecution2024}}, while risk-aware pathfinding\textsuperscript{\cite{nikolova2008stochastic}} and slippage models\textsuperscript{\cite{almgren2001optimal}} remain disconnected from executable planning. Our approach integrates these concerns into A* search with domain-specific heuristics.
