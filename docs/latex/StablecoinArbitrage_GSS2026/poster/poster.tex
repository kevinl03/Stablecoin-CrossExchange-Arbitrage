\begin{multicols}{3}

\posterheading{Motivation}
Prior arbitrage work stresses \emph{detecting} negative cycles, not \emph{executing} trades under fees, slippage, chain latency, and venue risk. Stablecoins ($>$ \$300B market cap) are ideal for cross-exchange routing because USD-pegged balances define a consistent notion of profit across venues.

\posterheading{Contribution}
\begin{itemize}
\item Cast stablecoin arbitrage as \textbf{A*} pathfinding on a \textbf{weighted directed graph} whose edges bundle realistic execution rates (trades + transfers).
\item Three \textbf{domain guidance} penalties $h_1$ (liquidity), $h_2$ (order-book slippage), $h_3$ (congestion/reliability) in $f\!=\!g\!+\!h$ (penalties, not admissible lower bounds), plus a parallel multi-start baseline.
\item Empirical study on a \textbf{CEX stablecoin dataset} (12 exchanges, live CCXT feeds), with cached graphs, scaling, an 8-hour campaign, and quote-staleness checks.
\end{itemize}

\posterheading{Graph model}
Nodes: \textbf{(exchange, stablecoin)}. Edges: \textbf{intra-exchange trades} and \textbf{cross-exchange transfers} of the \emph{same} symbol. Weight
\(
w(e) = -\log r(e)
\)
for effective rate $r(e)$ (fees, slippage, gas, delay, risk baked in). \textbf{Open goal:} any node with final USD value $>$ start; \textbf{no} requirement to return to the start token.

\columnbreak

\posterheading{Heuristics (summary)}
\begin{itemize}
\item $h_1$: penalize illiquid books (24h volume vs.\ order size and time left).
\item $h_2$: \textbf{slippage} from live depth / VWAP vs.\ mid; key result below.
\item $h_3$: chain time + static exchange reliability; uses weighted A* where needed.
\end{itemize}
Slippage:
\(
h_2 = \lambda_{\text{slip}} \max\{0, \text{slippage}_{\text{bps}} - \theta\}
\).

\posterheading{Key numbers (cached graph, \$10k)}
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrr@{}}
\toprule
Method & Succ. & Profit & Exp.\\
\midrule
Dijkstra / $h{=}0$ & 56.7\% & \$10.01 & 359\\
$h_2$ (slippage) & 56.7\% & \$9.91 & \textbf{256}\\
$h_1$ & 56.7\% & \$6.72 & 568\\
$h_3$ & 56.7\% & \$7.06 & 673\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
$h_2$ expands \textbf{29\% fewer} nodes than Dijkstra with profit within \textbf{1\%}. Overnight campaign (\textbf{7,200} instances): every A*-based run finds a profitable path; staleness: \textbf{99.6\%} still profitable up to 120\,s under delayed re-quotes.

\posterheading{Takeaway}
\textbf{$h_2$ (slippage)} best tracks optimal routes while pruning search. Liquidity and congestion penalties can \emph{over}-steer and hurt profit on the cached graph. Future: async order-book prefetch; DEX/AMM extensions.
